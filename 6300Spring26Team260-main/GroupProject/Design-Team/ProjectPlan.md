# ***Project Plan***

***Author:** Team 260*

## ***1\. Introduction***

***JobCompare6300** is an Android application designed to assist users in comparing their current job against potential job offers. The app utilizes a weighted scoring system to rank opportunities, allowing users to make informed career decisions based on their specific priorities.*

## ***2\. Process Description***

*The project development process is organized into a set of distinct activities. For each activity, the entrance criteria, specific tasks, and exit criteria are defined below.*

### ***Activity 1: Software Design (Deliverable 1\)***

* ***Description:** Each team member individually designs a UML diagram and submits a design description for the Job Comparison app, adhering to the requirements of Assignment 05\. The team then convenes to discuss the pros and cons of each individual approach. Based on this analysis, the team generates a final, unified design diagram and description.*  
* ***Entrance Criteria:***  
  * *Completion of individual designs based on Assignment 05 requirements.*  
* ***Exit Criteria:***  
  * *A consolidated design that incorporates the best features of the individual drafts.*  
  * *Submission of the Team Design Description (`design-description.md`).*  
  * *Submission of the Design Discussion Document (`design-discussion.md`).*  
  * *Submission of individual weekly reports.*

  ### ***Activity 2: Elaboration (Deliverable 2\)***

* ***Description:** Team members assume their respective roles to expand upon the project documentation. Max focuses on the Design Document, Lynne develops the Project Plan, Siddarth develops the Use Case Model, and Tom works on the Test Plan. Following individual work, the team meets to establish a holistic understanding of the system's full scope and ensure alignment.*  
* ***Entrance Criteria:***  
  * *Review of all requirements from Assignment 05\.*  
  * *Finalized team design from Deliverable 1\.*  
* ***Exit Criteria:***  
  * *Completion of individual sections for `ProjectPlan.md`, `UseCaseModel.md`, `DesignDocument.md`, and `TestPlan.md`.*  
  * *Peer review of all documents.*  
  * *Final approval and commitment of documents to the repository (no code implementation at this stage).*

  ### ***Activity 3: Software Construction (Deliverable 3\)***

* ***Description:** Based on the UML diagram and descriptions finalized in Deliverables 1 and 2, the team initiates the implementation of WiseJob. This phase focuses on developing the UI, implementing code logic, and integrating classes, packages, and maps. The team will hold regular meetings to refine the software as understanding of the system deepens.*  
* ***Entrance Criteria:***  
  * *Android development environment setup.*  
  * *Assignment of specific tasks to team members based on the revised design.*  
* ***Exit Criteria:***  
  * *A functional beta version of the application (WiseJob260).*  
  * *Updated documentation reflecting any changes.*  
  * *A test plan including initial test results.*

  ### ***Activity 4: Finalize (Deliverable 4\)***

* ***Description:** The team finalizes the application, generates the APK, and ensures strict compliance with all requirements. This phase involves completing all testing cycles and preparing the software for final release.*  
* ***Entrance Criteria:***  
  * *Functional beta version from Deliverable 3\.*  
  * *Initial test results and feedback from the Deliverable 3 testing phase.*  
* ***Exit Criteria:***  
  * *Final version of the application.*  
  * *APK file generated and placed in the APK directory.*  
  * *Test plan with final results.*  
  * *All documentation finalized and committed to the repository.*

  ## ***3\. Team***

*The following section outlines the team members and their assigned roles.*

### ***Roles and Responsibilities***

* ***Project Manager:** Coordinates team activities, manages the schedule, submits deliverables on Canvas, and ensures all team members remain on track. Facilitates communication and resolves conflicts.*  
* ***Software Architect:** Leads the design of the system architecture, maintains the UML class diagram, and ensures implementation aligns with the design. Reviews code for architectural consistency.*  
* ***Frontend Developer:** Designs and implements the Android user interface, including all screens (Main Menu, Current Job, Job Offers, Settings, Comparison). Ensures the UI is intuitive and responsive.*  
* ***Backend Developer:** Implements business logic, including job score computation, comparison settings, and data persistence using SQLite. Writes unit tests for core logic.*  
* ***QA Engineer:** Develops the comprehensive test plan. Executes unit testing (Tom and Siddarth) and integration testing. Conducts system and regression testing (Max and Lynne) to ensure the app meets all requirements prior to release.*
